## Decision and evidence scope

K2A qualification is complete as a bounded review, not an exhaustive history of these traditions.
Four individually bounded factual fragments are **QUALIFIED_FOR_KB**: one additive spelling value
and three calendar/cultural notes. No complete mansion, planetary-hour, Hurûf or Esmâ association
table is qualified. K2B source readiness is **YES, LIMITED** to those four fragments; a separately
authorized implementation must still enforce applicability, provenance, licensing and abstention.
Stage 11 knowledge readiness is **YES, LIMITED** to their descriptive narration, not a personalized
Kamerî reading. At K2A delivery no production KB existed; this research grants no Stage 11 authorization.
K2B now enforces the distinction: three Hijri fragments may be selected by month, while ASMA_NUM_001
is explicit reference-only and never selected from a personal abjad total of 66. No claims are promoted.

The [research ledger](references/kameri_interpretation_sources.json) is the claim-level authority:

These are bounded claim assessments, not 18 validated historical rules. Sixteen source records:
A:4 / B:11 / C:1. Access depth: 13 with relevant passages read, 1 thesis abstract only, 2
catalog records only (one directly opened, one search-index only). Neither catalogs nor an abstract
count as inspected rule tables.
Two Tier D discoveries are separately rejected, not included in the sixteen or used as rule sources.
The copyright guidance link is administrative evidence, not a traditional source.

Tier A preserves K0's meaning (primary work / institutional catalog), not a guarantee of textual
authenticity. The two Western primary-work transcriptions are noncritical, modern digital witnesses.
No remote source bytes were archived; source SHA256 is deliberately null. Access date and locators
support traceability, not immutable-snapshot reproduction. No existing raw evidence or hashes changed.

## Menzil: identity, historical context and bridge

[TDV ENVÂ'](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/enva) relates stellar rise/set traditions to seasons
and weather and provides a secondary mansion sequence. It does not edition-collate K0's proposed
Arabic labels. [Agrippa II.33](https://www.esotericarchives.com/agrippa/agripp2c.htm) provides
ordered names and activity/omen associations in a Western reception: the seventeenth is called
Alchil and associated, among other things, with buildings and seafaring. Agriculture, travel and
auspicious/harmful operations occur in that chapter; these are not modern personality traits.
The retained example is a deliberately partial research note, not an endorsed beneficial outcome.
Modern personality additions have not been traced to a dated textual origin: **UNVERIFIED**.

The primary witness discusses the eighth sphere and stellar references. Its printed degree
figures are not a replacement for K1's exact rational tropical partition. The Qazwini catalog
(A04) is a primary-manuscript lead, not independently inspected support for a theme.

An ordinal link could be defensible only as a new editorial convention, not a historical finding.
It would require complete name/order collation, selected tradition, versioned mapping, ambiguity
policy and explicit approval. Any such future link must say **PROJECT INTERPRETATION BRIDGE**.
For now `mansion_bridge_approved=false`: sector #17 does NOT select `MAN_INT_001`.
It must never imply that the Moon historically occupied that stellar mansion.

## Planetary hours

[Key of Solomon, Book I chapter II](https://www.esotericarchives.com/solomon/ksol.htm) contains
days-and-hours associations for the seven rulers. The ledger records selected friendship/writing/
navigation examples only. The digital editor identifies manuscript variants; Mathers combines
witnesses. Neither the historical author attribution nor a complete critical text is established
by this page. Preserve `clavicula_mathers_1888_i2_candidate_v1`, not “classical Islamic rule”.
The underlying context includes ritual magic, harmful aims and prescriptions: stripping these
away without a contextual limitation would misrepresent the source.

An academic publisher's description of E. M. Butler's *Ritual Magic* was a bibliographic lead only,
not an inspected corroborating passage, and is not counted in the ledger. Seven-ruler extraction,
edition comparison and a source-specific applicability review remain outstanding. Even if later
qualified, output can only state `historical_association`, never tell the user what to do this hour.

## Hurûf and Esmâ

[TDV HURÛF](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/huruf) discusses elemental and letter traditions,
but this review did not qualify a complete exact table. Ibn al-Arabî's *Fütûhât*, chapter 2, and
specific Bûnî witnesses are distinct primary leads, not interchangeable authorities.
[Gardiner (2012), pp.123–124 and 129](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/jais/volume/docs/vol12/v12_04_Gardiner_081-143.pdf)
documents problems of interpolation and coherence in the later *Şemsü'l-maârif el-kübrâ*.
A book title or a majority of copied modern tables cannot settle a disputed correspondence.
There is no admitted `GLOBAL_HURUF_TABLE`; every future system needs its own method/tradition ID,
complete table, locator, script policy and ambiguity policy. K1's existing number table is unchanged.

The [OMU thesis abstract](https://acikerisim.omu.edu.tr/entities/publication/f8f08308-ecbc-4337-a78b-7aaae458fabb)
describes mansion, planet, temperament, letter, zodiac and Asma headings in manuscript 05 Ba 1883/1.
This is evidence of a worthwhile historical lead, NOT an exact pair or full zodiac–Asma table.
The actual folios and selection direction were not read. Do not claim that all such associations
are modern inventions; equally, do not attribute today's internet lists to that manuscript.

[TDV EBCED](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ebced), Allah/hilal example and value table, supports
one bounded arithmetic fragment: `الله` → `1 + 30 + 30 + 5 = 66`. This is not a 99-name corpus,
a rule matching someone's name total, a destiny number or an instruction to repeat a name 66 times.
Article-wide character handling is not imported into ADR-017. No exact qualified personal-selection
or twelve-sign–Asma rule was found in the inspected corpus. Modern Tier D lists remain rejected.

Keep three categories separate: `abjad_numeric_value`, work-specific `associated_asma`, and the
prohibited claim that a person has a true/assigned divine name. Only the first has a qualified
specimen here. Product fields must not be called `personal_asma`, `true_asma` or `your_asma`.

## Dhikr, yıldıznâme and mother's name

[TDV ZİKİR](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/zikir) supplies devotional context, not a verified
personal count-selection method. A historically described practice would need its own exact
work/locator; `historical_practice` does not turn into `recommended_devotion`. The latter is
excluded regardless of later source discoveries. This review does not issue religious judgments.

[Çiftçi (2017), pp.202–203, notes 25–28](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/335978)
reports two methods from a 1997 Huseyni edition, p.49: one uses birth month/day with sign/ruler;
another combines own-name and mother's-name abjad in a twelve-based remainder procedure. The
primary page, complete tables, zero-remainder handling and spelling policy remain unverified.
No worked personal example is retained. A cataloged 1923 edition is not proof of the 1997 text.
[TDV YILDIZNÂME](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/yildizname) additionally distinguishes
birth-related and electional families; that taxonomy is not executable mechanics.

No hybrid fills gaps from another work. The ledger has separate candidates and missing-input/rule
lists. `mother_name_required=false` remains fixed: secondary evidence for a historical use is YES,
but necessity for an approved product method is NO. Future reconsideration requires complete source
qualification, separate authorization and privacy/data-minimization review. Do not collect it now.

## Hijri cultural context versus personality

Three bounded notes qualify: Ramadan's fasting/revelation context in
[Qur'an 2:185](https://kuran.diyanet.gov.tr/tefsir/Bakara-suresi/192/185-ayet-tefsiri), the
ramadaniyye literary category in [TDV RAMAZAN, section 2](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ramazan),
and pilgrimage/festival context in [TDV ZİLHİCCE](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/zilhicce).
These describe cultural associations, not a user's duty, merit, psychology or future.
They cover two months, not a complete twelve-month interpretation system. K1's tabular month
does not certify a historically observed or locally authoritative religious date.

Searches for Hijri-month personality and the inspected cultural sources yielded no qualified
birth-character method. This bounded negative result is not proof of universal nonexistence.
No inference from a month's religious significance to the character of someone born in it is allowed.

Future AI input is limited to calculated results + applicable qualified fragments + citations + policy.

## Rights and retention

Public access is not a reuse license. Historical works, translations, modern critical editions,
editorial notes and scans have separate rights. TDV's footer permits limited attributed quotation
with direct links, not wholesale articles/images. No quotes or scans are stored here; the ledger uses
short original factual summaries. Modern transcription copyright is not presumed absent because
the underlying work is old. University repositories and journal hosting likewise do not automatically
grant an AGPL-compatible prose-republication license.

[US Copyright Office Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf) distinguishes facts,
ideas and methods from protected expression. That is US guidance, not Turkish/worldwide clearance.
This stage avoids bulk extraction and does not certify blanket rights to any source or database.
K2B must recheck edition terms and jurisdiction before expanded extraction. Project AGPL remains
unchanged. Bibliographic author names are attribution metadata, not personal-input examples.
